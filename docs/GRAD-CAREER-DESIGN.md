# Adnan: from first tool to a company

The accepted product direction is a craftsperson/business strategy inside the
Brzo i Lokalno fictional world. City building is a retained secondary mode.
The game must identify the player before the first action. The initial caller
is Haris with the actual existing shelf request; the player is Adnan (wood skill).

The state machine is the source of truth. Phone messages are derived from job
creation/completion and delivered orders. Self-work triggers a client handover
call, never a call from the player to himself. No human communication or network
service is implied. Materials have actual purchase, production and consumption
entries. Career asset purchases and ongoing costs compete with job liquidity.

Workers remain independently available. Contractors bring equipment; employees
use owned reservable tool instances. Wear happens only during actual work. Low
condition, complaints, supplier route delays and unpaid overhead create decisions.
Original card/route/break games give bounded rest/learning outcomes. Field route
success executes one work step through the same existing work finalizer.

API boundaries: community.js is a closed deterministic rules module linked by
core.js, with versioned validation and migrations; community-ui.js uses the same
command port as the rest of the game. No eval, dynamically imported code, user SVG
or shell is used. Caller text, stock logs, card metadata and business data are
bounded/validated and rendered as literal text. Resource and acquisition effects
must be atomic. AI is not used or advertised in this game.

No existing main-platform app, PWA, private CLEA7 project or TECH source pin changes.
Publication uses the existing public TECH pull-request verification/deploy route.
Keep original author notices and artwork provenance. General mechanics such as
cards or resource allocation are implemented independently; no claim of global
novelty, universal legal clearance or AAA production scale is made.

## Hands-on work and visible career progression

The carpentry bench is available only for a booked wood job in the working state.
The player marks two measurements and fastens four points. Correct completion
uses the reserved real tool and the common work finalizer; two wrong hits cost
one material and six close the attempt. Each daily attempt is persisted. Closing
a job also closes an unfinished bench; normal day progression remains available.

The crew roster, progress screen, share text and daily report adapt to career
mode. Company milestones use the owned workshop, not a settlement-building
requirement. The original settlement specializations and construction rules remain
available in the explicit campaign mode. Income, expenses, debt, employees and
markets are read from actual state. There are no fabricated online players.

Pure-rule verification now includes a complete legal solo-to-regional-firm
progression: 34 completed jobs, two employees and three markets on game day 56,
without rescue money or any settlement buildings. It measures rule reachability,
not the enjoyment, duration or difficulty of human play.
